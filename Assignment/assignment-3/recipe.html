Cinnamon Roll Wreath with Apples

A delicious cinnamon roll wreath with apples.

Ingredients You'll Need

Crescent roll dough

Granulated sugar

Brown sugar

Ground cinnamon

Pazazz apples

Cream cheese

• Unsalted butter

• Milk

• Vanilla extract

• Powdered sugar

Step by Step Instructions

Step 1

Roll out the crescent roll dough and press the seams together.

Step 2

Mix butter, sugar, brown sugar and cinnamon together. Spread the mixture on the dough.

Step 3

Slice the Pazazz apple and place the slices on the dough.

Step 4

Roll the dough tightly into a tube.

Step 5

Slice the cinnamon roll tube in half lengthwise.

Step 6

Separate the two halves and make sure the apples are facing up.

Step 7

Carefully braid the two halves together.

Step 8

Form the wreath and bake at 350°F for 20–25 minutes until golden brown.

Source: Whitney Bond
